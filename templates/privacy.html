Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data — basic logs such as login timestamps, used to detect suspicious activity.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what you explicitly provide.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve Spendly. Specifically:

  • To display your expense history and summaries within the app.
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To send essential service communications (e.g. password resets).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We apply industry-standard practices including encrypted storage and secure connections (HTTPS). We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. We may use trusted infrastructure providers (hosting, databases) who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements. These providers are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You may also request a copy of your data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
